# Adopters

Does your organization use Kubescape's reusable workflows? We welcome community
adopters to share how they use this repository.

To add your organization, open a pull request updating the table below with your
organization name, website, and a short description of your use case. A public
contact for questions is optional. Share only information your organization has
approved for public publication. Keep entries in alphabetical order by
organization name.

Every commit must include a DCO sign-off, for example:

```sh
git commit -s -m "docs: add organization to workflows adopters"
```

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

For adoption of the broader Kubescape project, see the central
[Kubescape adopters list](https://github.com/kubescape/project-governance/blob/main/ADOPTERS.md).
